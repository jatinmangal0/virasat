VIRASAT

Our Story

Hello! We're the students behind Virasat.

We are a group of students. Virasat began as a project. Somewhere along the way it stopped being "the project" and became something we actually care about, a lot.

Here's the honest part: none of us come from a coding background. So the street Gaju walks down, the letter that arrives with a city's stamp on it, all of it was something we had to figure out from scratch.

We've had a lot of meetings. Many rounds of ideas. And almost every meeting brought a new struggle.

That's really our whole story: struggle, learn, repeat. We kept going because of what Virasat is about.

[WHY HERITAGE: in your own words, a moment one of you felt close to, or far from, your own roots]

India's cities are full of stories, food, festivals, art and languages that families have carried for generations. We wanted a place where a student could walk into a city, maybe one they've never seen, maybe one their family comes from, and feel that it belongs to them too.

[WHY UDAIPUR AND KOLKATA: why these two cities came first]

So here is our invitation. Walk the street. Pick a city. Learn something.

Then tell us what's wrong or missing. We're students, and we will get things wrong. If we got something about your culture, your language or your city wrong, please correct us. That's how we learn the next thing.
