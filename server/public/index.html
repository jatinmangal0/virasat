What is Virasat?

Virasat means heritage. This is a small, playful place to learn about two great Indian cities. You pick a city, Gaju walks you there, and a letter arrives with that city's stamp on it. Inside are its palaces and bridges, its festivals, its food and its stories, and a few questions at the end to press your seal into the wax.

Students, travellers and curious people of every age are welcome, no account needed. Sign your passport if you want Virasat to keep your stamps and scores.

Udaipur

Marble palaces on mirror lakes, in the old kingdom of Mewar.

Kolkata

Durga Puja, College Street's books, trams and the great bridge over the Hooghly.
