What is Virasat?

Virasat means heritage. This is a small, playful place to learn about two great Indian cities. You pick a city, Gaju walks you there, and a letter arrives with that city's stamp on it. Inside are its palaces and bridges, its festivals, its food and its stories, plus a quiz to see what stuck.

Students, travellers and curious people of every age are welcome. Make a free account and Virasat keeps your passport: the cities you have opened and your best quiz scores.

Udaipur

Marble palaces on mirror lakes, in the old kingdom of Mewar.

Kolkata

Trams, yellow taxis, Durga Puja and the great bridge over the Hooghly.
